About us

We kit out the crews that build Dubai.

XpertOne Prints is the safety wear and uniform arm of XpertOne Creative. We hold stock in Al Quoz, print in-house, and deliver across the Emirates.

What we actually do

Most of our customers come to us with the same problem: they need a few hundred pieces of workwear, with a logo on it, on site by the end of next week — and their current supplier either quotes on a six-week import lead time or cannot print at all.

We solve that by holding the standard lines in stock and doing the printing ourselves. Hi-vis vests in five colours and three grades, cotton and twill pant & shirt sets from 190 to 260 GSM, cargo trousers and coveralls, safety shoes, gloves, eye and hearing protection, harnesses, rainwear and road safety equipment. If you need something we do not list — a specific colourway, a certified line, a site-branded set — we source it.

Orders start at ten pieces. There is no upper limit that has caused us trouble yet.
