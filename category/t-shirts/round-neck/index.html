A few things, made clear.

Is logo printing included?

Yes. Front and back logo printing is included in the displayed AED 30 cotton, AED 35 dry-fit and AED 40 custom / dual-colour shirt prices. We confirm artwork and placement before production.

Does round-neck cost the same as polo?

Yes. Cotton round-neck and cotton polo are AED 30 each. Dry-fit round-neck and dry-fit polo are AED 35 each.

Which sizes can I order?

S, M, L, XL, 2XL, 3XL, 4XL and 5XL. Enter your size quantities in the studio. Sales confirms measurements and availability before ordering.

Is my preview the final production design?

No. Mockups illustrate the range, and the interactive preview helps communicate your idea. Fabric shade, cut, panel construction and print scale are confirmed in an artwork proof before production.
